# Teacher Guide: Online Exam Simulation

**Date:** Friday, 13 November 2026  
**Time:** Homeroom Session (07:20 to 07:40)

---

## Part 1: Read These Rules to Students First

Before students open their browsers, explain these four points clearly:

1. **Why we are doing this:** Parts of the upcoming semester exam, specifically the Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ), will be done online. Today is a short simulation so everyone knows how the exam page works.
2. **Do not leave the exam screen:** The exam page uses anti-cheating protection. Students cannot open other apps or switch windows while the exam is running.
3. **Restricted keys:** Tell students not to press `Esc`, `F11`, or `Alt + Tab`. Pressing any of these keys locks the exam screen right away, and the student cannot continue until unlocked.
4. **4-Strike Lockdown Rule:**
   * **1st lock:** Warning only (no time penalty).
   * **2nd lock:** 5 minutes deducted from exam time.
   * **3rd lock:** 10 minutes deducted from exam time.
   * **4th lock:** The exam submits automatically, and the student cannot continue.

---

## Part 2: Guide Students Step by Step

Walk the class through these steps in order:

1. **Open Microsoft Edge.**  
   Check that every student uses **Microsoft Edge** (not Chrome or another browser).
2. **Open the exam website.**  
   Have students type this address into the browser address bar: `s.id/icmexam`
3. **Enter the simulation code.**  
   On the exam welcome page, tell students to type: `PILOT`
4. **Click `START EXAM`.**
5. **Fill in student details.**  
   Students type their **Name** and **Class**. Tell them to keep **Candidate ID** empty.
6. **Start the simulation.**  
   Once ready, students click `BEGIN EXAM NOW`.
7. **Complete and submit.**  
   Students answer the questions according to the on-screen instructions and submit their work when done. Remind them again not to press `Esc`, `F11`, or `Alt + Tab`.
